# 600 — Tenets

## For a 9-year-old curious mind

1. **600 words** — one great story per day, not homework
2. **5 power words** — words you can actually say to teachers, friends, and family
3. **Tap to answer** — multiple choice, encouraging voice if wrong, clear hint on 2nd miss
4. **Green tick** — finish the quiz, own the day
5. **Monday → Sunday** — week rhythm that matches school
6. **3 PM unlock** — something to look forward to after school
7. **White & black** — clean, bold, app-like on tablet and phone
8. **No repeats** — fresh words and stories tracked forever

## Wrong answer flow

1. **First wrong** → warm voice: "Not quite! Try again — check the passage."
2. **Second wrong** → hint spoken + shown: "The story said X and Y — the answer is **Z**."

## Design principles (kid UX)

- Big tap targets (52px+) for thumbs on tablets
- Add to Home Screen = feels like an app (PWA)
- Natural voice (Samantha / Google UK Female) — not robotic
- Celebrate completion with green ✓ on the day pill
- Curiosity hooks → ask grandparents questions
