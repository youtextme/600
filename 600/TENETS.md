# 600 — Tenets

Six hundred words. Every day. One curious mind.

## The promise

Each day at **3:00 PM Korea time**, a new essay unlocks. Each week on **Sunday at 8:00 AM**, seven fresh essays appear — titles visible, content waiting.

## What Ayaan gains (without feeling taught)

1. **600 words of real reading** — story-first science, history, and nature; never a lecture
2. **5 advanced words** — usable in daily speech, with pronunciation on tap
3. **3 grammar patterns** — absorbed through repetition, not rules drilled
4. **Deep curiosity** — hooks that send him to grandparents, parents, and his own research
5. **Comprehension discipline** — find answers in the text; hints when stuck; report card on first-try vs retry

## Design principles

| Tenet | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Scientific** | Claims are true; wonder follows evidence |
| **Logical** | Because → therefore; questions have paths to answers |
| **Fresh eyes** | No preconceived sermons; the world stays strange and worth probing |
| **Effortless** | Reads like a story you'd choose, not homework you endure |
| **No repeats** | Words, topics, and essays tracked — the vault remembers what he already met |
| **Habit** | Seven greyed titles ahead; tomorrow is always waiting |

## The rotation

- **Sunday 8 AM KST** — GitHub Action generates the week's seven essays
- **Daily 3 PM KST** — today's essay unlocks (client-side, Korea time)
- **Progress** — stored locally: quiz scores, first-try count, share message

## Share

One tap copies a ~100-word WhatsApp message — formatted, proud, ready for family.
